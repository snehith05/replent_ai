# Warranty and terms

Every phone carries the manufacturer's 12 month warranty. We add 6 months of in-store service at no extra cost.

Out-of-warranty screen replacement is charged at 2,800 rupees for mid-range models and 7,500 rupees for flagships. Battery replacement is 1,600 rupees.

## Exchange

Working devices under 3 years old are accepted in exchange. Exchange value is assessed in store and ranges from 1,200 to 18,000 rupees.

## Business terms

Our standard supplier payment term is 30 days. We hold a dealer licence issued in 2016 and renewed in 2024.
