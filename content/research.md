# Project Tidewatch

Project Tidewatch began in March 2019. It uses 48 solar-powered buoys moored between 2 and 11 kilometres offshore to record water temperature, salinity and wave height every 15 minutes.

## Team and funding

The project is led by Dr. Arvind Kulkarni with a team of 6 engineers. Its total budget to date is 3,600,000 rupees, most of it from a state coastal-resilience grant.

## Findings so far

Across 2019 to 2025, average surface temperature at the buoys rose by 0.4 degrees Celsius. The buoys have logged 212 high-wave events, and 17 of those led to early warnings for local fishing boats.
