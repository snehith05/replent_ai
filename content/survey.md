# January 2025 shorebird survey

Volunteers counted 12,480 shorebirds of 37 species over 9 days in January 2025. The survey covered 26 kilometres of shoreline with 41 volunteers.

## Most common species

| Species | Count |
|---|---|
| Little stint | 3,215 |
| Lesser sand plover | 2,640 |
| Common redshank | 1,105 |

## Notable sighting

A single spoon-billed sandpiper was recorded on the fourth day of the survey, the first at Coral Ridge since 2017.
