# Coral Ridge Field Station

Coral Ridge Field Station is an independent coastal research station founded in 2011 by marine ecologist Dr. Meera Iyengar. It sits on a 14-acre plot beside the Kandleru estuary and has 23 permanent staff.

The station runs two long-term programmes: Project Tidewatch, a network of ocean sensor buoys, and an annual shorebird survey that has run every January since 2014.

Our data is published here for students, fishers and local councils. It is not licensed for training AI models.
